Research Framework

AI-Powered Urban Heat Island (UHI) Mitigation

Dual-stage deep learning platform integrating Satellite Semantic Segmentation (U-Net) and Surface Thermal Regression (ANN) for counterfactual urban greening interventions.

🎯 Scientific Motivation

Rapid urbanization alters natural surface energy balances by converting pervious vegetative canopies into impervious asphalt, concrete, and rooftops. This induces elevated Land Surface Temperatures (LST), increasing cooling energy burdens and human heat stress.

This platform enables urban planners to upload high-resolution satellite imagery, extract pixel-level surface composition, forecast thermal anomalies, and simulate strategic greening interventions (10% to 50% conversion of impervious surfaces).

■ Class 0: Impervious ■ Class 1: Vegetation ■ Class 2: Bare Soil ■ Class 3: Water

⚙️ Neural Network Pipeline

  • [1]
    U-Net Semantic Segmentation: DoubleConv blocks, skip connections, multi-class Softmax classifier trained to segment high-resolution optical imagery.
  • [2]
    LST Regression ANN: Deep Multilayer Perceptron [128 → 64 → 32] mapping 4 land-cover fractions + 3 environmental variables to temperature anomalies.
  • [3]
    Counterfactual Greening Engine: Morphological clustering algorithms converting selected impervious pixels into vegetation canopy to forecast temperature reduction.
